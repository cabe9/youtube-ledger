YouTube Ledger

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: September 6, 2026

This policy describes how YouTube Ledger, the Chrome and Firefox extension maintained by cabe9, handles information.

Your activity stays in your browser profile. YouTube Ledger processes and stores YouTube activity locally. The extension does not automatically send that activity to the developer, an analytics service, or an AI provider. No account is required.

Information the extension handles

When tracking is enabled, the extension reads information from supported YouTube pages and stores the following in your browser profile:

  • Viewing activity: video titles, channel names, video IDs and video URLs; available channel addresses and portrait-image URLs; first and last activity timestamps; locally generated session identifiers; and elapsed time for foreground playback, background audio, silent background playback, paused or buffering activity, browsing, and detected ads.
  • Recommendation activity: actions such as showing or hiding recommendations, detections of recommendations visible while YouTube has focus, timestamps, locally generated event identifiers, and the relevant YouTube page path or video ID.
  • Video entry points: a Ledger group link or supported observed recommendation, search, subscription, Watch Later or channel link, or a supported native autoplay transition or explicit Watch Later playlist playback; the group ID and name at launch when applicable, explicit queue transitions, and predecessor visit/video identifiers for observed viewing journeys. Unobserved entry points are marked unknown. These are included in viewing history and review exports.
  • Information you enter: purpose labels, daily notes, and optional instructions for exported review prompts.
  • Channel groups: group names, locally generated group identifiers, channel membership, channel names and IDs, canonical channel URLs, available portrait-image URLs and portrait lookup times, and group creation/update times, ordering, collapse/filter/sort preferences, last group visit times, hidden-video IDs, and any symbols, emoji or local images you choose. Large GIFs are resized locally; uploaded icon files are not sent to a server. These are saved when you use the group controls, including while activity tracking is paused.
  • Preferences: settings such as the dashboard theme, animation preference, recommendation visibility, and whether tracking is paused.

The extension also reads playback position, playback speed, audio status, and page focus to calculate activity durations. It also stores unique played position ranges, video duration, the last recorded playhead for Resume, and manual watch-state overrides to show Started/Watched status. It does not record video, audio, screenshots, passwords, cookies, or activity on unrelated websites. Private or incognito use is disabled.

How the information is used

Information is used to provide the local viewing history, daily and period summaries, recommendation controls and activity counts, labels, notes, and exports you request. The extension does not use your information for advertising, profiling for third parties, or selling data.

On the Watch Later page, Ledger can read the loaded video rows and YouTube’s visible progress bars to help you select watched videos. Selecting “Remove selected” operates YouTube’s native removal controls for those videos in your signed-in YouTube account. Ledger does not read account cookies or call a private account API for this feature. Selection stays in the page, is cleared on navigation, and is not uploaded to Ledger or an AI provider. Cleanup does not delete your Ledger viewing records and does not remove videos automatically after playback.

Channel groups organize creators locally. When you add a channel from an address or supported YouTube page, Ledger requests the public YouTube channel or video page to resolve the creator's ID and name. These requests go directly to YouTube without account cookies. Group names, membership, and viewing history are not uploaded with these requests. Group actions do not change your YouTube subscriptions. Opening a group feed or member list can also request public channel pages to find or update channel portraits, through the same paced request queue as upload feeds and video details, with one request at a time. Successful portraits are checked at most weekly and failed lookups at most daily.

Opening a group feed, and periodic checks of saved groups while a YouTube tab is visible, request public upload feeds directly from YouTube without account cookies, and display thumbnails served by YouTube's image hosts. Ledger caches video IDs, titles, creator IDs and names, publication timestamps, available total durations, live/upcoming status, YouTube Shorts classification, and lookup/refresh times locally. The feed and membership controls remain available while activity tracking is paused.

Visible group cards can request public watch-page metadata directly from YouTube without account cookies to learn a video’s total length, live/upcoming status, or YouTube Shorts classification. These lookups run at most two at a time, stop after reading the player metadata when possible, and are cached with upload records. They do not open a player or add Ledger watch records. Available durations are normally refreshed at most weekly; live and upcoming status is checked more often while visible, and failed lookups are retried at most hourly.

Storage and retention

Records and preferences are stored using the browser's local extension storage (storage.local). The extension does not send them to a developer-operated server or use browser sync storage. Chrome and Firefox profiles keep separate histories.

History, Review and group controls display available video thumbnails and channel portraits from YouTube image hosts. These image requests go directly to those hosts without a page referrer; they do not go through a Ledger server. Images load as needed, and missing images have a text or placeholder fallback. Portrait URLs and available channel addresses are included in profile backups.

Viewing records have no automatic expiration schedule. They remain in that profile until you delete them, remove the extension's stored data, or uninstall the extension. The upload cache keeps up to 250 videos per channel and 5,000 overall, favoring newer releases. A channel's cache is removed when it no longer belongs to any group. Storage is managed by your browser and device; this policy does not describe any separate device backup or browser service you use.

Temporary group-link and observed-source contexts are valid for up to two hours. They are pruned when another feed context is created and cleared from extension session storage when the browser session ends. They associate random link tokens with a group and its displayed videos or an observed link source and destination video. Group queues store a snapshot of up to 500 videos and explicit navigation steps for up to 24 hours, with at most eight queues. Undo stores up to 20 recent changes and any removed channel cache needed to restore them for five minutes; larger snapshots are compressed locally and older records may be evicted sooner to limit storage. These records expire and are cleared when the browser session ends. No account playlist is created. Per-tab session storage preserves feed searches, scroll positions and an observed video entry point across navigation or reload; entry-point state is cleared when tracking is paused. Tokens are URL fragments and are not included in HTTP requests to YouTube, but a page script can read its URL fragment.

Recording saves use local retry receipts and observed wall-clock playback intervals to avoid duplicate seconds and distinguish playback from pauses. A failed save is retried while its YouTube tab remains open, with at most 300 unsaved samples held in that tab’s memory. If recording cannot be saved, Ledger displays a warning and toolbar badge. Local upload metadata may be evicted to make room for recording; history, notes, groups and icons are not automatically deleted. Operational warnings may use browser session storage when local storage is full. None of these records are sent to a server.

Your controls and deletion

  • Pause tracking stops saving new activity and recommendation events. It does not delete existing records.
  • Delete this day removes that day's recorded activity, recommendation events, and purpose labels. It currently does not remove the day's notes; erase those in the Notes field separately.
  • You can edit or clear notes and custom review instructions in the dashboard. Restoring default settings does not delete viewing history or daily notes.
  • The Groups tab lets you rename or delete a group and remove individual channel memberships. Removing a channel from one group keeps memberships in other groups. Deleting groups does not delete viewing history.
  • To remove all local extension data, remove its stored data through your browser's available controls or uninstall the extension. The separate watch-progress record remains when a single history day is deleted. Files you previously exported remain wherever you saved them until you delete those files yourself.

The developer cannot access or delete records that remain only in your browser profile.

Exports and third-party services

JSON exports and LLM review-prompt exports are created locally as downloadable files. Depending on the export, they can include viewing activity, recommendation events, labels, notes, preferences, dates, and the time zone reported by your browser.

Full backup files also include groups, chosen icons, upload cache, hidden-video preferences, last visit times, resume positions and watch state. Temporary link, queue and undo contexts are excluded. Restore validates a selected backup and replaces standard Ledger data only after you choose Restore. A recovery backup downloads first. Files can be moved manually between Chrome and Firefox; there is no automatic synchronization.

No automatic LLM or AI service connection is included. If you choose to upload, paste, or send an export to an AI provider or anyone else, that recipient receives the information you share and handles it under its own policies. Exporting a file by itself does not send it to the developer.

Permissions

The storage permission saves your records and preferences locally. Access to www.youtube.com and m.youtube.com allows the extension to observe supported playback and page activity and show or hide recommendation elements. It does not request access to unrelated websites, your general browser history, your microphone, or your camera.

This policy page and external websites

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes requests to serve the page under its privacy statement. This page contains no analytics scripts, remote fonts, or embedded trackers. Visiting it does not give GitHub or the developer access to the extension's locally stored history.

YouTube, your browser provider, and other websites or services you choose to use operate separately under their own policies.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when the extension's information-handling practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest policy update. Its source and revision history are available in the project repository.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the YouTube Ledger issue tracker. Issues are public, so do not include private viewing history, notes, exports, or other sensitive information.
