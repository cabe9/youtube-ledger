# YouTube Ledger privacy policy

Read the current policy at **[cabe9.github.io/youtube-ledger/privacy.html](https://cabe9.github.io/youtube-ledger/privacy.html)**.

YouTube Ledger processes and stores activity locally in your browser profile. The policy explains the information handled, its uses, storage and deletion, optional exports, permissions, and how to contact the maintainer.
